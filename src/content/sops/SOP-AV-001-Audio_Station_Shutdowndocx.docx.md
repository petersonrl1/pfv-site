## **Overview**

The Allen & Heath SQ6 is a professional digital mixing console. Digital mixers store settings in memory, and cutting power without a proper shutdown can corrupt those saved scenes — much like force-quitting a computer. Following this procedure every time protects the equipment and ensures the board is ready for the next service or event.

## **Before You Begin — Pre-Shutdown Checklist**

Confirm all of the following before starting the shutdown:

|  ☐  | The event/service is fully over and no one is still using audio                        |
| :-: | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  ☐  | All microphones and monitors are no longer needed — you are ready to mute all channels |
|  ☐  | Any recording or streaming has been stopped                                            |
|  ☐  | You have saved the current scene (if any changes were made — see Step 2\)              |

## **Shutdown Steps**

Follow each step in order. Do not skip steps.

| **1** | **Mute or lower all channel faders** - _Push the soft button labeled MUTE ALL that is to the right of the touchscreen. This prevents a loud pop through the speakers when the board powers down._                               |
| :---: | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **2** | **Save your scene (if you made any changes)** _Press SCENES on the touchscreen → select the current scene → tap STORE. If no changes were made, skip this step._                                                              |
| **3** | **Press the Processing button to see the Shutdown button** _The Processing soft button (bottom-left of touchscreen) until Shutdown appears. This may take a few presses, but eventually the Shutdown button will show_        |
| **4** | **Confirm the shutdown on the touchscreen** _A dialog box will appear asking "Are you sure?" or "Shutdown?" — tap YES or OK to confirm. The board will save its state and power off safely, and the light bar will turn off._ |
| **5** | **Power off the amplifiers and active speakers** _Flip white power switch to OFF — directly under the mixer desk, top of amp rack. This shuts off board, amps, and IEM receivers in the correct sequence._                    |
| **6** | **Do NOT power off any other sound equipment** _Leave the wireless receivers and RF Venue box on. DO NOT turn key that is at the top right of the amp rack_                                                                   |
| **7** | **Secure the equipment** _Place the dust cover over the mixer which should be stored along the right side of the mixer console during use_                                                                                    |

| ✓ You're Done\! The SQ6 is now safely shut down. Your scenes and settings have been saved. The board will be ready to go for the next service or event. |

## **Troubleshooting**

Common issues and what to do:

| Problem                                         | What to Do                                                                                                                       |
| :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Touchscreen doesn't respond when I tap it       | Wait 10 seconds — it may be processing. If still frozen, contact AV Lead before pulling power.                                   |
| No shutdown dialog appeared after holding power | Hold the button for a full 3 seconds. If still no response, try pressing and holding again. Do not cut power without the dialog. |
| Board won't turn on after shutdown              | Check that the power strip is on. Check the power cable in the rear. Wait 30 seconds and try again.                              |
| I accidentally cut power without the dialog     | Note it happened and tell your AV Lead. The board usually recovers but saved scenes may have been affected.                      |
